About this overview

These figures describe {{ player.full_name }}'s {{ season }} season aggregate currently stored locally. For an in-progress season, they reflect the data available at the most recent import and do not imply the season is complete.

If the player appeared for more than one club, the stored line is the combined season aggregate; this page does not model the individual team stints.

AVG is hits per at-bat. OBP is (H + BB + HBP) / (AB + BB + HBP + SF). SLG is total bases per at-bat, and OPS is OBP + SLG. A rate shown as — is undefined because its denominator is zero, which is not the same as .000.

K%, BB%, and HR% each divide by plate appearances, so the bars describe how the player's plate appearances ended. They are not a ranking or a quality score, and nothing here is compared with MLB.
